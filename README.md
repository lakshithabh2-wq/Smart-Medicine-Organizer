# Smart-Medicine-Organizer
A prototype project to aid elderly with taking medication on time. 

  The main problem that arises as a person grows older is the increase in medications. Even though, they take multiple medications, remembering when to take a small pill is a challenge for many senior citizens over the age 85. Even though an alarm on a phone may seem like a simple solution, many elderly citizens struggle with handling technology. 

  Therefore, I was able to design a prototype of a medicine box which contains spots for lights to blink. The blinking lights will be able to get the senior citizen's attention and doesn't require day to day input to keep it functional. Additionally, the audible reminder aides in helping those who struggle with poor vision. 
 
  The physical enclosure was designed using FreeCAD. The box contains 4 individual cavities for medication with its own hinged lid. The LED is placed right in front of its cavity to prevent confusion. The box is a bright teal with clear lids, so medicine is visible through the top, but the box is easily spotted. 
  
  Although this prototype alerts when to take a certain medication it has its limits, specifically with how to change the input time for when the lights need to go off. Additionally, the box could be larger to be able to hold more medicine. I aspire to eventually make it so that the senior citizen can take a picture of the prescription and automatically edit the timings for each box.
